MODEL NOTES

Transparent by design

Payer

Premium − incurred claims − admin − quality investment − illustrative MLR floor adjustment.

Baseline medical PMPM already includes the modeled provider and drug pathways. Only their changes are added, avoiding double counting.

Provider

Completed cases × allowed rate × collection yield − variable cost − fixed cost + platform savings − transition cost.

Completed cases are limited by capacity. A payer-rate change is a provider receipt and payer expense, not a system-wide gain.

Life sciences

Treated × units × list price × (1 − rebate) − COGS − operating investment.

A separate Phase I asset value uses probability-weighted illustrative future contribution, not current product earnings.

Scope limits: one synthetic payer product, one provider pathway, one marketed drug, one Phase I asset; no actual contracts, tax, risk adjustment, provider payer mix, PBM fees, claims runout, discounting of future cash flows, clinical efficacy curve, vendor-side P&L, or patient-level advice. The MLR adjustment is a teaching proxy, not a statutory filing calculation. Drug rebate is modeled as 100% passed to this payer; real contracts vary.

Sources & assumption register

The displayed results are calculated locally from synthetic inputs. Regulatory and benchmark callouts below are external anchors; most slider starting values and ranges are illustrative teaching assumptions, not estimates for an actual payer, hospital, or medicine. No live data is fetched.

  • CMS, Medical Loss Ratio — MA/Part D floor of 85%; reviewed October 2026.
  • Congressional Research Service, Medical Loss Ratio — ACA commercial floors of 80% individual/small group and 85% large group; reviewed October 2026.
  • MedPAC, March 2026 hospital report — FY2024 hospital all-payer operating margin 6.5%, not a target for this synthetic provider pathway.
  • BIO / Informa / QLS, 2021 — 7.9% Phase I-to-approval likelihood across 2011–2020 development programs; historical and indication-dependent.
  • CBO, 2025 340B report — drug-channel transfer economics; 340B is discussed but not simulated in the v1.
  • CMS National Health Expenditure — broad system-spending context; not used as a local input default.

Algorithm: deterministic annual P&L with event-volume changes and cross-ledger cash transfers. Baseline inputs are illustrative and held constant for scenario deltas. No causal inference or actuarial validation is claimed.
